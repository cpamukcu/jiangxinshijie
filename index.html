→ Well Solution

Steel vs. Aluminum Alloy Derricks

Steel structure derricks are cost-effective — the outer cladding is finished later by your own contractor to match any interior style, with an anti-rust coating for lasting durability.

Aluminum alloy derricks use an independent glazed frame — no extra footprint, better light transmission, with industrial-grade aluminum and laminated safety glass for a longer service life.
