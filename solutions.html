Steel or Aluminum Alloy Derricks

The shaft is the part of the elevator your family sees every day — so it is worth choosing deliberately.

Steel structure

Cost-effective. The outer cladding is finished later by your own contractor to match any interior style, and an anti-rust coating protects the frame for lasting durability.

Aluminum alloy

An independent glazed frame with no extra footprint and better light transmission. Industrial-grade aluminum and laminated safety glass give it a longer service life.
