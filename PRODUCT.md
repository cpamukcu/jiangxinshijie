# Product

**What:** SINNVO (星沃) designs, decorates, manufactures and installs custom villa/home elevators in Huzhou, China. Sales, R&D, manufacturing and installation under one roof.

**Audience:** overseas villa owners, architects/interior designers, and distributors evaluating a Chinese manufacturer. They judge on finish quality, fit for their home (no pit, low headroom, door layouts) and safety.

**Job of the site:** make a distant buyer trust the craft enough to send shaft dimensions and request a quote.

**Mode:** Persuade (marketing site).

**Brand tone (client brand copy):** China-chic, young, fashionable, designer brand. Name = 星 star + 沃 fertile/abundant. Positioning: premium villa elevators where every cabin tells a story (Eastern and Western design heritage).

**Brand commitments (pinned by client, not open):** white base, violet #7D33FF single accent, near-black #141223, Outfit + Inter, official wordmark and Star Badge. Feels like a luxury residential-architecture brand, not a factory catalogue: calm, spacious, confident.

**Proof available:** real photos of SINNVO cabins, showroom and factory; brochure structure diagrams, cabin collections, finish swatches; 4 shaft/drive structures; 8 safety systems; fully custom cabins.

**Never:** invented facts, founding year, partner/competitor brand names, certifications, counts, awards, client names. Old brand name anywhere.
