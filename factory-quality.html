The Same Attention for Every Cabin

Every panel is hand-finished and quality-checked before it leaves the workshop, whether it is a single handrail or a full cabin.

Cabin decoration and non-standard home-lift manufacturing is where SINNVO started, and it is still the core skill behind every finish in the Collections.

Every Cabin Tells a Story

Our cabin designs draw on Eastern and Western heritage, from porcelain, the Baoxiang flower and Ru-kiln celadon to the lines of the Parthenon, and turn it into stone, metal and wood panels made in-house.

Sourced, Finished and Assembled Under One Roof

Sintered stone, brushed and mirror steel, walnut panel, laminated safety glass and industrial-grade aluminum all pass through the same workshop that engineers the shaft structure — so materials and structure are designed together, not bolted on afterward.
