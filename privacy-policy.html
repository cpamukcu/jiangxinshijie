Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026. This page explains what happens to the information you submit through this website.

→ 1. Who We Are

Huzhou Jiangxinshijie Elevator Co., Ltd.

No. 1888, Renrui West Road, Nanxun Economic Development Zone, Huzhou City, Zhejiang Province, China. This policy applies to this website only, not to any third-party site it links to.

→ 2. What We Collect

Only What You Submit Through the Enquiry Form

This site does not require an account and does not run advertising or tracking cookies. The only personal information we collect is what you choose to enter into the enquiry form on the Contact page: your name, company, email, phone/WhatsApp number, country, product interest, and the message you write, including any shaft dimensions, floor count, budget or timeline you share.

Submitting the form opens your device's own email application with that information pre-filled, addressed to our sales inbox. The message is only sent if you then choose to send that email yourself.

→ 3. How We Use It

To Respond to Your Enquiry — Nothing Else

We use the information you submit only to answer your enquiry, prepare a quotation, and follow up about a potential order. We do not sell, rent or trade it to third parties, and we do not use it for purposes unrelated to your enquiry.

→ 4. Analytics & Cookies

None Are Currently Active

This website does not currently run Google Analytics, advertising pixels, or non-essential cookies. If analytics tools are added in future to understand site usage, this page will be updated first to describe what is collected and how to opt out.

→ 5. Data Storage & Retention

Kept Only as Long as Needed

Enquiry details you email us are stored in our business email account and kept for as long as reasonably needed to handle your enquiry and any resulting order, or as required by law, after which they are deleted.

→ 6. Your Rights

Ask Us to Access, Correct or Delete It

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us through the Contact page or emailing us directly. We will respond within a reasonable time.

→ 7. Changes to This Policy

We'll Update This Page, Not Just Our Practices

If how we collect or use information changes — for example, if analytics are added — we will update this page and change the date above. Continued use of this website after an update means you accept the revised policy.
