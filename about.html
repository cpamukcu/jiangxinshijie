About Jiangxinshijie

Built inside the elevator industry. Refined for the home.

Since 2018 we have made the parts of an elevator people actually see and touch. Today we design, build and install the whole villa elevator around them.

→ Our Story

From a Single Decorative Panel to a Legacy of Elevators

Jiangxinshijie began in 2018 at the most demanding link of the elevator supply chain: cabin decoration and non-standard home-lift manufacturing. Years of working to the standards of the world’s largest elevator brands built deep expertise in structure, materials and space.

That experience has been proven on landmark projects — from the Tajikistan National Theatre to premium hotels and hundreds of private villas — and it is the foundation of every home elevator we build today.

Understanding manufacturing is only half the craft. Understanding a home is the rest — which is why each elevator is designed to become part of the house, not a box hidden inside it.
