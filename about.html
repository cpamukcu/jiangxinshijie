From a Single Decorative Panel to a Legacy of Elevators

SINNVO began at the most demanding link of the elevator supply chain: cabin decoration and non-standard home-lift manufacturing. That work built deep expertise in structure, materials and space, and it is the foundation of every home elevator we build today.

Understanding manufacturing is only half the craft. Understanding a home is the rest — which is why each elevator is designed to become part of the house, not a box hidden inside it.
