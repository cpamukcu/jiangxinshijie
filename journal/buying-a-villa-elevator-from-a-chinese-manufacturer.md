Ordering a home elevator from a factory on the other side of the world is a big step. It can make sense: you deal with the people who design and build it, and every cabin can be made for your home rather than chosen from a fixed range. But you need the right answers before you commit.

These are the questions we suggest asking any manufacturer, with SINNVO's answers where they apply.

## Who actually makes it?

Ask whether you are talking to the factory or a trading company. SINNVO handles sales, R&D, manufacturing and installation under one roof, in its own workshop in the Nanxun Economic Development Zone in Huzhou, Zhejiang. You can see the workshop on the [Factory & Quality](factory-quality.html) page.

## Will it be designed for my shaft?

A good quote starts from your measurements, not a catalogue model. SINNVO has no fixed model numbers. Once you send the shaft dimensions, floor count and preferred structure, we prepare the cross-section, layout drawings and technical specification for your unit as part of the quotation. The [measuring guide](journal-what-to-measure-before-you-ask-for-a-quote.html) lists what to send.

## Is it approved where I live?

Rules for home elevators differ by country and sometimes by city. Before you order from any supplier, ask your local building authority or elevator inspector what certification and inspection the installation needs. Then ask the manufacturer for the documents that prove compliance. Do this first, not after the crates arrive.

## Who installs it?

Ask who will install the elevator in your country and who commissions it. Commissioning matters: call panels, safety systems and door interlocks should be tested floor by floor before handover. Find out who you call for service afterwards and how spare parts reach you.

## How is it packed?

An elevator travels by sea for weeks. SINNVO wraps, boxes and foam-packs panels inside wooden export crates before they leave Huzhou. Ask any supplier to show you photos of their export packing.

## What should the quote include?

Make sure you know whether the price covers the cabin and structure only, or also shipping, installation, import duties and local approval. Compare quotes on the same basis.

## How fast will they reply?

Slow answers before an order rarely get faster after one. SINNVO usually replies within two business days, by email or WhatsApp.
