A home elevator is used by everyone in the house: grandparents, guests, small children who think the call button is a toy. These are the questions families ask us most, and the systems that answer them. All eight are built into every SINNVO elevator.

## What happens if the power goes out mid-ride?

The elevator levels itself automatically to the nearest floor and opens the doors so passengers can step out. Nobody waits between floors for the power to come back.

## Can the doors close on someone?

An intelligent light curtain watches the doorway. It reacts in milliseconds and without contact, so the doors stop before they touch a hand, a bag or a pet.

## How do I stop small children using it alone?

The child-safety lock uses function-key access control to keep children from riding unaccompanied.

## What if someone needs help inside the cabin?

A built-in landline interface gives one-touch calling to a contact you choose, such as a family member or the building's staff.

## Can it be watched remotely?

Yes. The elevator can connect to SINNVO's IoT platform for real-time remote monitoring.

## Is it noisy?

Silent brake-control technology and a high-performance drive chip keep the ride smooth and quiet, which matters when the shaft stands next to a bedroom or living room.

## Does it use power when nobody is riding?

An intelligent sleep mode cuts standby power without slowing the response when someone calls the cabin.

## Do we have to touch the buttons?

Smart touchscreen call panels support contactless calling and show a live floor display and load readout. Standard and optional panel series are available.

## Checked before it leaves the workshop

Safety also comes from how the elevator is built and installed. Every panel is hand-finished and quality-checked before it ships. On site, call panels, safety systems and the door interlock are tested floor by floor before the elevator is handed over.
